Platform

Everything needed to run attendance properly.

Hardware, identity and records in one place — so administrators spend their morning reading a dashboard instead of reconciling a register.

01

Biometric verification

Enrol a fingerprint once and match it at the terminal in milliseconds. No shared PINs, no borrowed cards, no proxy clock-ins.

02

RFID attendance

Issue card credentials to staff, contractors or visitors and record every tap against the right profile.

03

Live attendance

Watch clock-ins arrive from every terminal as they happen, and filter by person, branch or status.

04

People management

Create profiles, attach credentials and keep a clean record for every person — onboarding through to offboarding.

05

Device management

Register a terminal in minutes and keep track of connection status, firmware state and pending commands remotely.

06

Reports

Produce daily and monthly registers for administrators, managers and payroll — exportable in one click.

How it works

Built around your organisation.

Connect attendance hardware to a central platform and keep every identity and every event in one place.

  1. 01

    Register people

    Add staff, students or contractors with their fingerprint and RFID credentials attached to a single profile.

  2. 02

    Verify identity

    Every clock-in is matched against that profile at the terminal — one person, one record, no sharing.

  3. 03

    Track attendance

    Events flow into the dashboard in real time, filtered by branch, department or status and ready for reporting.
